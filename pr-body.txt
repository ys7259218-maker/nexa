## What & why
The placeholder notification bell is now a real notification center. "Notifications (coming later)" is a working link with a live badge count straight from your stored data — no separate table, nothing to keep in sync.

Notifications are derived at request time from the signed-in user's records, in priority order:

- **AI drafts pending review** (danger) — outbound `draft_blocked` messages waiting for a human.
- **Open conversations** (info) — WhatsApp contacts that may be waiting for a reply.
- **AI employees with no WhatsApp channel** (warning) — a channel must be linked before an employee can handle inbound traffic.

### Changes
- New `lib/notifications.ts`: parallel, RLS-scoped reads; badge count reflects `NotificationItem[]`.
- New `app/notifications/page.tsx` (server component, authenticated) + `app/notifications/loading.tsx` shell. Empty state: "You are all caught up".
- `DashboardHeader` bell is now a link to `/notifications` with a red count badge (`aria-hidden` on the badge; the link's `aria-label` announces the count).
- Dashboard page computes the count once and threads it into the header.
- `Sidebar` gains a global **Notifications** entry so the center is reachable from every page.

### Security & scope
- All reads use the cookie session; RLS scopes notifications to the owner. No service-role key.
- Derived, request-time state means badges always match the data exactly.
