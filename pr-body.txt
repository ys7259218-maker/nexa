## Changes
The login and signup forms now cross-link to each other:
- Login form: "New to Nexa? Create an account" → `/signup`
- Signup form: "Already have an account? Sign in" → `/login`

Previously neither form offered a path to the other, so new users could dead-end on login and existing users were stuck after confirming their account.

## Notes
- Display-only change; no auth logic touched.
- Matches the cyan link treatment used by "Forgot password?" on the login form.
- Contract test asserts both forms expose the opposite destination.
