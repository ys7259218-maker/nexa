## Changes
Every authenticated app page was rendering **two `<h1>` elements**: the page title and the "Nexa" brand word in the workspace sidebar. This PR moves the sidebar brand out of the heading hierarchy (`<h1>` → `<p>`, styling unchanged), so each page now exposes exactly one `<h1>` — its real title.

Verified the other `<h1>` usages are the single per-page titles (Dashboard, AI Employees, Conversations, settings, auth forms, legal pages, onboarding) or isolated branches in the conversations page; the New AI Employee form renders its own `<main>` without the sidebar, so it stays single-headed too.

## Notes
- Accessibility-only change; zero visual impact (same classes on the brand text).
- Contract test locks it: the sidebar must keep its "Nexa" brand outside `<h1>`.
