Companion to the inbound webhook ledger: an **outbound history** page that lists every message this workspace sent, newest first, with per-status filters.

## What changes
- `lib/outboundHistory.ts`:
  - `OutboundStatusFilter = "all" | "sent" | "delivered" | "read" | "failed" | "draft_blocked"`.
  - `parseOutboundStatusFilter(value)` — validates unknown search-param input, defaulting to `all`.
  - `listOutboundHistory(client, filter, limit)` — RLS-scoped read of the owner's `messages` where `direction = outbound`, newest first, optional status filter. Uses the signed-in client (no service-role key).
  - `previewBody(body, maxChars)` — single-line body preview truncated at a word boundary.
- `app/outbound-history/page.tsx` — status filter chips, per-message rows (status chip, template/type tag, masked wamid, body preview, sent time), empty and error states.
- `components/dashboard/Sidebar.tsx` — "Outbound history" link.

## Privacy
- Sender-facing ids (`wa_message_id`) are masked; no service-role access — rows are scoped to the signed-in owner.
