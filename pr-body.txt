## Summary

Ships the approve-and-send path for AI-drafted WhatsApp replies, closing the last
honest "disabled" gap in the traffic menu. Drafts stay `draft_blocked` until an
owner explicitly approves them; approving triggers a real, fail-closed WhatsApp
send and persists the outcome.

## What changed

- `lib/server/draftSender.ts` — `sendApprovedDraft(service, sessionUserId, messageId)`: loads the message and conversation through a service client, then **manually enforces ownership and safety before any side effect** (message must be the user's own outbound `draft_blocked` draft; conversation must be the user's own, not opted out, and not under human takeover; stored number must be valid E.164; sender must be outbound-enabled). Returns structured outcomes: `not_found`, `not_draft`, `not_allowed`, `not_ready`, `invalid_recipient`, `send_failed`, `persist_failed`, or `ok`.
- `app/api/outbound/draft/route.ts` — authenticated POST `{ messageId }` (401 without a session, 413 body cap, 400 bad id, 503 not ready/persist failure, 404 hidden, 403 for declined sends).
- `components/conversations/DraftSendButton.tsx` — client "Approve & send" button shown only on pending AI drafts; reports outcome inline and `router.refresh()`es on success.
- `app/conversations/page.tsx` — computes `outboundReady` from env and changes the pill to `Outbound enabled` vs `Outbound disabled`; draft bubbles show the approval button only when enabled.
- `supabase/migrations/20260905120000_outbound_sent_status.sql` — re-issues the `messages.status` check constraint to allow `sent` (only ever written after a successful real send; all other statuses unchanged). Mirrored in `docs/migrations/20260905_outbound_sent_status.sql`.
- `lib/conversations.ts` — `MessageStatus` gains `"sent"`.
- Tests: 10 new workflow tests (fail-closed matrix) in `lib/outbound/whatsappSender.test.ts`; new UI-contract test covering the page wiring, workflow guards, route, button, and migration; migration-chain test updated for the new file. Local `npm run check` green.

## Safety

Every send is gated: outbound must be configured (env), the recipient must be a
valid E.164, the conversation must be eligible, and the row is only marked `sent`
after Meta accepts the message. Nothing sends automatically — a human approving
the draft is the only trigger. 24-hour session window, templates, and inbound
delivery-status wiring remain explicitly deferred as documented in `whatsappSender.ts`.
