## Changes
The onboarding preview had no way to tell where you are in the flow: `StepDots` existed as dead code (defined, never rendered), and a step change dropped focus to the page body for keyboard and screen-reader users.

- `StepDots` is now rendered on every step (4 dots, current step highlighted wider).
- A screen-reader announcement follows each step change: `Step X of 4`, and the decorative dots are `aria-hidden` so they stay silent.
- Each step transition refocuses the step container (`tabIndex={-1}`, `preventScroll`) so keyboard and screen-reader users keep their position instead of being dropped to `<body>`.

## Notes
- Code-only; the landing page (`app/page.tsx`) renders the same `OnboardingFlow`, so the dots appear on `/`.
- The preview still sends nothing; no behavior other than focus/progress semantics changed.
