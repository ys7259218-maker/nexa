## Changes
Every navigation landmark on the site now has a distinct accessible name. Screen-reader users navigating landmarks (common shortcut: "d" in NVDA, or the Landmarks rotor in VoiceOver) previously heard `navigation` twice inside the authenticated shell, and an unnamed `navigation` on legal pages.

- Navbar top bar: `Workspace shortcuts`
- Sidebar: `Primary workspace navigation`
- Legal page cross-links (`Privacy Policy` / `Terms` / `Data Deletion`): now labelled `Legal pages`
