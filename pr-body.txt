## Bound the pending-approvals queue and scope failed-sends conversation lookups

Two support queries on the work-queue pages were still unbounded: approvals
pulled every unanswered draft, every conversation, and every inbound row, and
failed-sends loaded every conversation regardless of the queue. This slice
bounds them to exactly what the queues need.

### Pending approvals (`lib/pendingApprovals.ts`, `app/pending-approvals`)
- Drafts capped at `PENDING_APPROVALS_LIMIT = 500`, newest first.
- Conversations looked up only for the drafts shown (`.in("id", conversationIds)`),
  never the whole table.
- Inbound scan window-bounded (`INBOUND_WINDOW_SCAN_MS` = service window); an
  inbound older than the window can never open the 24-hour window, so window state
  is unchanged.
- New result shape `{ approvals, total, truncated }` from an exact head/count, so
  the real backlog is never hidden by the cap. The page now shows
  "N drafts waiting for approval · showing the newest M" when trimmed.

### Failed sends (`lib/failedSends.ts`)
- `listFailedSends` reads the failed sends first, then fetches conversations only
  for those conversation ids (`.in("id", conversationIds)`). With no failed sends,
  no conversations query runs at all. The retry path is unaffected — each candidate
  is still re-verified per-message by the guarded sender.

### Tests
- `pendingApprovals.test.ts` covers the bounded three-query shape, exact total +
  truncation, and window semantics. `failedSends.test.ts` covers the scoped
  conversation lookup and the new error path. Both suites' mocks now model
  `in`/`limit`/`gte`/head-count chains.
- `uiContracts.test.ts` pins the caps, the exact-count shape, the truncation note,
  the window-bound, and the `.in(...)` conversation scoping.
