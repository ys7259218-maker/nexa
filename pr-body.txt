## Changes
Adds a themed 404 page (`app/not-found.tsx`). Previously the app used Next's built-in not-found, which renders in its own light styling that clashes with the app's dark theme. The new page:

- matches the app's dark theme (`bg-black`, zinc palette, cyan accent)
- shows one honest `<h1>` ("This page could not be found")
- offers a single "Back to Nexa" home link
- exposes its own metadata title ("Page Not Found | Nexa AI")

## Notes
- Display-only change; routing/status-code behavior (404) is unchanged.
- Contract test locks the theme, single-heading rule, metadata title, and the way-home link.
