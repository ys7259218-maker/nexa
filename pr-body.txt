## Changes
Dates that users see are now also machine-readable via the `<time dateTime>` stamp, matching what the issue-reporting page already did:

- Conversation inbox: per-chat last-message time, message timestamps, and the "source turns" timestamps inside a draft disclosure.
- Team settings: each member's "Joined …" date.

Screen readers and assistive tools can now parse the exact instant instead of only the localized string.
