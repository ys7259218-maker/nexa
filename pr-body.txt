## Changes
The reset-password form now offers a "Back to login" link, matching the recovery flow on the forgot-password form. Previously a user who opened a recovery link by mistake (or finished resetting) had no in-page way back — the form only redirected automatically on success.

## Notes
- Display-only change; no auth logic touched.
- Reuses the exact "Back to login" copy and styling from ForgotPasswordForm.
- Contract test asserts both recovery forms expose the return path.
