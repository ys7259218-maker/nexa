## Changes
Two small display-honesty fixes on the dashboard:

- **Recent Calls** no longer shows a trailing chevron on each row. The rows are read-only displays (no call-detail route exists), so the arrow was implying a tappable next step that does nothing. Removing it so the affordance matches the behavior.
- **Upcoming Appointments** now stamps the scheduled date with `<time dateTime>` (machine-readable), matching the inbox, team, and issue-reporting timestamps.
