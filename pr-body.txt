A delivery-health view that complements the outbound history: how outbound messages travel **sent → delivered → read**, with exact failure/delivery/read rates.

## What changes
- `lib/deliveryFunnel.ts`:
  - `computeDeliveryFunnel(messages)` — buckets terminal outbound statuses (`sent`/`delivered`/`read`/`failed`) and computes delivered, read, and failed rates over the attempted total. Non-terminal statuses (e.g. `draft_blocked`, `received`) are excluded so drafts don't skew rates.
  - `getDeliveryFunnel(client)` — RLS-scoped load of the owner's outbound message statuses (no service-role key, no LIMIT, so rates are exact).
  - `deliveryStageLabel(stage)`.
- `app/delivery-funnel/page.tsx` — stat cards (attempted, delivered rate, read rate, failed rate) plus a proportional funnel bar per stage with per-stage percent and count; empty and error states.
- `components/dashboard/Sidebar.tsx` — "Delivery funnel" link beside Outbound history.
